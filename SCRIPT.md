# REZON – Harman Hackathon 2026 selection video: script

Single source of truth for the story, voiceover, captions and timing.
Language: Hungarian. Target length **1:56** (hard cap 1:58; the rules allow max 2:00).
30 fps. All times are mm:ss.

**Voices**
- **VO** = Kaszti (narrator).
- **DEV** = the Rezon device speaking. Also Kaszti, run through a "small speaker" filter.
- **TEAM** = each member's own line.

**Characters**
- **Mari néni**: 81, lives alone.
- **Anna**: her daughter.

## 4. TEAM (1:32 – 1:56)

Format: a pop-up book.
- Each member **pops up** from the center fold with a paper "flap" sound.
- Their **photo head sits on a cardboard suit body**, and the mouth flaps on their line.
- A name/role tag sits below them.
- After the line they **slide right** and stay there, so the members stack left to right.

## How the video answers the jury's questions

| Their question | Where |
|---|---|
| Problem / opportunity | S02–S05 |
| Human–machine relationship in 5 years | S07, S11 (the house speaks and asks) |
| Role of sound | S11 device voice, S09 the floor "hears", tagline |
| What's new vs today | S03 camera fails, S02 pendant out of reach, S08–S09 no camera, no wearable |
| Team introduced | S14–S19 |
| Technical depth (selection criterion) | S08 exploded view, S09 tape, S10 detection rule |

**Technical claims (approved by Kaszti, 2026-10-21):** wireless, battery-powered, speaker in the unit, "nem vesz fel hangot" (doesn't record audio), "impact + stillness = fall".
