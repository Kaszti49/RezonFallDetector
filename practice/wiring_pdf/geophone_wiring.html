Click pins AN, RX, TX, SCL, SDA, 5V stay empty. The two Click GND pins are joined on the board: the right GND (right row, 8th · mikroBUS 9) needs no wire to the ESP32. It is only used as a handy ground for CH1− (wire 13). “Left/right” = Click seen from the component side with AN and PWM at the top; the names printed next to the pins decide.

Max two wires per screw terminal. If your terminals are labelled AIN0P / AIN0N / AIN1P / AIN1N, then CH1 = AIN0, CH2 = AIN1. If you can’t tell + from − on the geophone, it doesn’t matter for this test: the trace is just inverted.

Check before USB: 3.3V on the Click goes to 3V3, never VIN/5V. If the Click has a supply jumper (VCC SEL / 3V3–5V), set it to 3V3. Stand the geophone upright (spike down) on the table or floor and don’t hold it while testing.

Run it

  1. Arduino IDE → board ESP32 Dev Module. Open practice/geophone_test/geophone_test.ino, upload. No libraries needed.
  2. Optional: Serial Monitor at 460800 → press EN. Expect ID = 0x22xx and then lots of D,… lines. Close the Serial Monitor afterwards.
  3. Open practice/geo_viz/index.html in Chrome or Edge → Connect ESP32 → pick the COM port.
  4. Tap the table 30 cm from the geophone, walk past it, drop a book.

The Demo button in the page plays fake footsteps and a fall, so you can try the page before the wiring is done.

If it doesn’t work

  • ID = 0x0000 or 0xFFFF: check SPI (wires 3–6), RST (7) and especially PWM → TX2 (8). No clock means no answer.
  • Page shows 0 samples/s: INT → D4 (wire 9).
  • CRC errors > 0: wires too long or loose; keep jumpers short.
  • Flat line while you stomp: wires 11–13, the resistor, and the terminal screws.
  • “clipping” in the log: lower the ADC gain in the page (default ×4 → ×2 or ×1).
  • Constant 50 Hz hum: wire 13 missing (CH1− not grounded).
