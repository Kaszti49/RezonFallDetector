Sensitivity V/(m/s)

SM-24: 28.8 V/(m/s) open, about 20.9 V/(m/s) with the 1 kΩ resistor across the coil. All thresholds are mm/s in the band. A hit counts when it beats both the minimum and N × the room’s background (it adapts to noisy rooms). Hit ≥ impact → Impact; smaller hits are Step / tap; 3+ footfalls 0.35–1.6 s apart → Walking (hits closer than 0.35 s count as one footfall; the first 1.2 s after an impact is ignored as ringing). Impact, then no walking (4 steady footfalls) within that time → FALL-LIKE; small movements after a fall don’t cancel it. Steps are detected on the step path (18–60 Hz, steady building hum subtracted), impacts on 10–45 Hz. Defaults tuned on recordings 1–3: about 97 % of steps found, about 0.2 false hits/s while lying still, all 7 known falls right.
